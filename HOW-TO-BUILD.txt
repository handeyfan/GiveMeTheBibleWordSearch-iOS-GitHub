Give Me the Bible Word Search: iPhone and iPad (and Apple-silicon Mac) build
============================================================================

This folder is a ready-made Xcode project. An .ipa file can only be produced and signed on a Mac with
Xcode and your own Apple Developer account, so the final step has to happen on your Mac.

What you need
- A Mac with Xcode 15 or newer (free from the Mac App Store).
- An Apple ID. A free Apple ID can install the app on your own iPhone/iPad for 7 days at a time.
  The paid Apple Developer Program ($99/year) is needed for permanent installs, TestFlight and the App Store.

Steps
1. Unzip this folder and double-click GiveMeTheBibleWordSearch.xcodeproj to open it in Xcode.
2. Click the project in the left sidebar, choose the "GiveMeTheBibleWordSearch" target, open
   "Signing & Capabilities", tick "Automatically manage signing" and pick your Team (your Apple ID).
   If Xcode says the bundle identifier is taken, change com.kjv.givemethebiblewordsearch to something unique,
   e.g. com.yourname.givemethebiblewordsearch.
3. To try it: plug in your iPhone or iPad, choose it at the top of the window and press Run (the play button).
   (On the device, the first time: Settings > General > VPN & Device Management > trust your developer profile.
   iOS 16+ also needs Settings > Privacy & Security > Developer Mode turned on.)
4. To make the .ipa / publish: choose "Any iOS Device (arm64)" at the top, then Product > Archive.
   In the Organizer window press "Distribute App" and pick App Store Connect (App Store / TestFlight),
   Ad Hoc (an .ipa for registered devices) or Development.

iMac / Mac
- Apple-silicon Macs (M1 and newer) can run this same iPhone/iPad app ("Designed for iPad"):
  in Xcode choose "My Mac (Designed for iPad)" and press Run; on the App Store it can be offered to Mac users too.
- For any Mac, including Intel iMacs, use the separate macOS app included with this release
  (GiveMeTheBibleWordSearch-macOS-AppleSilicon.zip or -Intel.zip). No Xcode needed.

What the iOS wrapper does
- Shows the bundled app (the www folder) full screen. Everything works offline.
- Print uses the iOS print sheet; exporting highlights opens "Save to Files"; Share opens the share sheet.
- Exit: Apple does not allow apps to close themselves, so Exit saves your progress and returns to the title screen.
- Progress is stored on the device per player, the same as on Android and Windows.

Note: this project was written without access to a Mac, so it has not been compiled or run on an iPhone yet.
If Xcode shows an error, copy its message and send it, and it can be fixed.
